How to join

In order to join, please read the instructions.

The server runs Minecraft Forge 1.20.1 with a small set of required mods. Follow every step below in order.

Install the CurseForge app
Download it from curseforge.com/download/app and sign in.
Create a custom profile
Minecraft version 1.20.1, modloader Forge.
Add the required mods
Grab the mod list from our Discord and drop them into the profile's mods folder. Or just simply take our finished Profile Folder and all you do is press Start, simple as that.
Launch and connect
Hit play, then Multiplayer → Add server → nationsmp.net.
NOTE Joining without the required mods will disconnect you on login. Get the mod list in Discord first.
Category 01

Nations & Towns

Land, government, and diplomacy run on Towny.

Founding a town

/town new [name] claims your first plot. Invite residents, set taxes, and grow.

Forming a nation

Once your town is established, unite with others under /nation new [name] to pool land, defense, and diplomacy.

Category 02

Economy

Every job feeds the same currency. What you earn mining, farming, or trading pays for land, weapons, and vehicles.

Earning

Take a job — mining, farming, woodcutting — and get paid per action, with pay scaling as you level up.

Spending

Buy and sell through the player shop, or trade directly with other towns.

Vehicles

Category 04

Rules

Please read and respect these core rules carefully before joining.

1. Ideology & Conduct

Ingame, follow your ideology. If it is extremism, that is okay for roleplay purposes, but you are not allowed to use racial slurs under any circumstances.

2. Zero Tolerance for Threats

Any kind of threats, like doxxing or disturbing behavior, will be punished by a Community Ban with no chance of appeal.

3. No Unfair Advantages

X-Ray and the use of any third-party advantage is forbidden. This counts for cheats as well. Shaders or custom textures are allowed as long as they do not give an unfair advantage like X-Ray.

4. Reporting Players

If you want to report someone, do not DM staff members. Please use the Ticket System and always provide evidence.
